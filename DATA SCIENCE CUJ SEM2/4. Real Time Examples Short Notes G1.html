1. Real-Time AI Applications

Aaj kal hum jo bhi use karte hain, usme Artificial Intelligence peeche se real-time mein kaam kar rahi hoti hai. Yahan kuch popular examples hain:

Facebook News Feed

Agar aap kisi friend ki posts ko regularly "like" karte ho, toh algorithm samajh jata hai. Agli baar jaise hi wo friend kuch post karega, Facebook automatically uski activity aapki feed mein top par dikhayega.

Netflix ML (Machine Learning) ka use karta hai. Jo movies aapne pehle dekhi hain aur rate ki hain, unke basis par Netflix predict kar leta hai ki aapko aane wali (unwatched) movies pasand aayengi ya nahi.

Machine learning algorithms purani aur nayi paintings ko analyze karke ye bata sakte hain ki kis artist ka influence kis dusre artist par raha hai, jo pehle insaan recognize nahi kar paate the.

Social Media (Q&A)

Platforms (jaise Quora, Reddit) par koi bhi question pooch sakta hai. AI ensure karta hai ki jin answers ko sabse zyada "Upvotes" milte hain, wo filter hoke top par show hon.

3. ChatGPT & Generative AI

Late 2022 mein Generative AI ka boom aaya. Generative AI purane AI se thoda alag hai. Purana AI data ko sirf classify ya recognize karta tha, jabki Generative AI nayi (fresh) chizein create karta hai.

What is ChatGPT?

Yeh ek Artificial Intelligence tool hai jise OpenAI ne late 2022 mein banaya tha.

  • Built on a Natural Language Processing model.
  • Iska core model hai Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT).
  • Trained on Vast Data: Isko bohot bade dataset par train kiya gaya hai.
  • Text Patterns: Yeh text ke patterns ko pehchanta hai. "You Type It, It Responds".

Generative vs Discriminatory

Dono approach alag hoti hain:

  • Discriminatory (TAR): Technology Assisted Review. Ye NLP ka use patterns samajhne ke liye karta hai, sirf data ko classify karne ke liye (e.g., spam vs not spam).
  • Generative: NLP aur OpenAI ke 'Transformer' model ka use karke bilkul naya content create karta hai. (e.g., writing a poem or code).
*Fact: As of 2023, Global Generative AI market is worth $13.71 Billion.*

4. Deepfakes Architecture (Trick or Treat?)

Deepfakes AI ka use karke ek person ka face kisi doosre person ke face se swap kar dete hain. Iska basic architecture Encoders aur Decoders par based hota hai.

The Architecture Process

Imagine do faces hain: Face A (Original) aur Face B (Target).

1. Training Phase

Algorithm dono faces ko alag-alag encode karta hai.
Original Face A ➔ Encoder ➔ Latent Representation of A
Original Face B ➔ Encoder ➔ Latent Representation of B
Phit in representations ko unke apne Decoders (Decoder A & Decoder B) se pass karke face wapas reconstruct kiya jata hai model ko train karne ke liye.

2. Generation Phase (The "Fake" part)

Aab magic hota hai. Face A ki latent representation ko Decoder B mein daal diya jata hai.
Latent Representation of Face A ➔ Decoder B ➔ Reconstructed Face B from A
Is tarah Face B ke expressions, Face A jaisa dikhne lagta hai.

Behind the Scenes: AI Math Formulations

1. The Transformer (GPT) Attention

Self-attention mechanism that allows models like ChatGPT to understand context:

$$\text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$

Where Q = Query, K = Key, V = Value matrices.

2. Encoder-Decoder (Deepfakes)

Latent space mapping function where $x$ is input face and $\hat{x}$ is reconstructed face:

Encoder: $$z = f_\theta(x)$$
Decoder: $$\hat{x} = g_\phi(z)$$

Where $z$ is the latent representation transferred across decoders.

3. Basic Machine Learning Core

Foundation of all AI (Weights, Biases, and Activation):

$$y = \sigma(Wx + b)$$

Where $\sigma$ is activation, $W$ is weight matrix, and $b$ is bias.

4. GenAI Probability

Predicting the next word based on previous words (Conditional Probability):

$$P(w_t | w_1, w_2, ..., w_{t-1})$$

GPT tries to maximize this probability for human-like text.
