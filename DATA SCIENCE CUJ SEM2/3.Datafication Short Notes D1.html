2. डेटाफिकेशन (Datafication)

🔁 परिभाषा: "taking all aspects of life and turning them into data" – जीवन के हर पहलू (ऑनलाइन/ऑफलाइन, real-time/past) को डेटा में बदलना।

📦 उदाहरण: सोशल मीडिया एक्टिविटी, लोकेशन, शॉपिंग, सेहट – सबका डेटा बनना।

💡 वैल्यू क्रिएशन: "Once we datafy things, we can transform their purpose and turn information into new forms of value." – नए प्रोडक्ट, सर्विस, इनसाइट्स।

🚀 ड्राइवर्स: सस्ती कंप्यूटिंग पावर, communication capacity, छोटे सेंसर (IoT), behavior-environment-data का फीडबैक लूप।

4. डेटा साइंस (परिभाषा)

📌 DS: manages, manipulates, extracts, interprets knowledge from tremendous data. Multidisciplinary, addresses big data challenges, principles apply to all data (big & small).

🔬 शामिल फील्ड्स:

  • Computer Science – pattern recognition, visualization, data warehousing, HPC, DB, AI
  • Mathematics – mathematical modeling
  • Statistics – statistical & stochastic modeling, probability

📋 दूसरी परिभाषा: technical skills पर फोकस।

5. डेटा साइंटिस्ट

6. स्किल्स & टाइप्स

📚 Concentration in DS: Mathematics, Applied Statistics, Programming (R, Python, SQL), Data Mining, Database Management, Machine Learning.

👥 Types of Data Scientists: Machine Learning Scientist, Statistician, Software Programming Analyst, Data Engineer, Actuarial Scientist, Business Analytic Practitioner, Quality Analyst, Spatial Data Scientist, Mathematician, Digital Analytic Consultant.

🎓 Academia vs Industry (O'Neil & Schutt):

  • Academia: trained in discipline, works with large data, computational problems, solves real-world problems.
  • Industry: extract meaning, interpret data (stats+ML tools + human), collects/cleans/munges data, EDA, builds models, communicates findings with visualizations.
